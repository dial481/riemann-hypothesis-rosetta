# Notice

The reader notes in SPINE.md and `analysis/reader-notes.tsv` were generated by an AI inspecting source code without compiling it. They are starting points for expert review, not authoritative mathematical descriptions.

The structural analysis (import graph, centrality rankings, community detection, critical paths) is mechanical and reproducible. The one-line descriptions of what each module does are not. A mathematician may find that a note misidentifies a construction or misses the point of a module entirely. Corrections are welcome via issues or pull requests.
